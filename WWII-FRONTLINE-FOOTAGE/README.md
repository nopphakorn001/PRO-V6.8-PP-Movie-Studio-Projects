# WWII FRONTLINE FOOTAGE

Historical AI cinematic reenactment series covering 10 major World War II battlefields from 1939–1945.

Production standard:
- PRO V6.8 / P&P Movie Studio STEP6-ready JSON
- 9:16 vertical
- 15 scenes × 8 seconds ≈ 120 seconds per episode
- cinematic wartime-footage aesthetic
- period-correct uniforms, vehicles, weapons and environments
- no spoken dialogue
- no narrator
- ambience, battlefield SFX and restrained score only
- non-graphic combat
- 1 scene = 1 primary action + 1 camera movement
- preserve terrain, weather, uniforms, dirt, damage and prop continuity from scene to scene
- generated reenactment, not archival footage

Episodes:
1. Poland 1939 — The War Begins
2. Dunkirk 1940 — Waiting on the Beach
3. Eastern Front 1941 — Into the Vast Land
4. Moscow 1941 — Winter Resistance
5. El Alamein 1942 — War in the Desert
6. Stalingrad 1942–43 — City of Fire
7. Guadalcanal 1942–43 — Jungle Frontline
8. Normandy 1944 — Omaha Beach Assault
9. Bastogne 1944 — Surrounded in Snow
10. Berlin 1945 — The Last Streets
