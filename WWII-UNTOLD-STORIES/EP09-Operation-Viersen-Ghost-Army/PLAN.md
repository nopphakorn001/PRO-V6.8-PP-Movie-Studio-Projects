# EP09 — กองทัพปลอมริมแม่น้ำไรน์

**Event:** Operation Viersen / 23rd Headquarters Special Troops (Ghost Army)  
**Date:** 18–24 March 1945  
**Place:** Lower Rhine, Germany  
**Target:** 75 scenes x 8s ≈ 10 min, 9:16

## Hook

ก่อนกองทัพที่ 9 ของสหรัฐข้ามแม่น้ำไรน์ ทหารราวหนึ่งพันนายถูกสั่งให้ทำสิ่งตรงข้ามกับการซ่อนตัว: สร้างกองทัพขนาดมหึมาที่ไม่มีอยู่จริงให้ฝ่ายเยอรมันมองเห็น ได้ยิน และตรวจจับทางวิทยุ

## 5-act structure

- **Scenes 01–15 — The Rhine problem:** Ninth Army prepares to cross; secrecy around the real crossing area, strength and timing is critical.
- **Scenes 16–30 — Building a phantom army:** introduce 23rd Headquarters Special Troops, inflatable vehicles, sonic deception, fake radio networks, insignia/traffic impersonation.
- **Scenes 31–45 — Viersen begins:** fake convoys move, assembly areas are staged, real and inflatable equipment create visual credibility, radio operators imitate two divisions.
- **Scenes 46–60 — Sound of an invasion that is not coming:** construction and artillery soundtracks, smoke and visual activity imply preparations for a Rhine crossing; German observation/fire reacts to the deception area.
- **Scenes 61–75 — Real crossing + assessment:** actual 30th and 79th Infantry Divisions assemble elsewhere and cross in the true sector; explain evidence that Viersen materially aided deception and earned the unit a Ninth Army commendation.

## Principal roles

- Col. Harry L. Reeder — commander of 23rd Headquarters Special Troops.
- Lt. Col. Merrick H. Truly — linked to planning/coordination of Viersen.
- Camouflage artists, radio operators and sonic crews — small source-grounded ensemble.
- Narrator — primary storyteller.

## Locations

Rhine-side fields, fake assembly areas, inflatable vehicle staging, radio trucks, sonic trucks, roads with phony convoys, real crossing sector shown only after deception setup.

## Accuracy notes

- Official/declassified history dates Viersen 18–24 March and states the objective was to deceive the enemy about crossing area, strength and timing.
- Congressional findings describe roughly 1,100 men impersonating about 40,000 / two divisions using fabricated radio networks, sound and hundreds of vehicles.
- Avoid claiming all German dispositions were caused solely by Ghost Army; present documented assessment that the operation materially assisted the wider deception.
- Inflatable tanks should look convincing at observation distance but remain physically lightweight when shown being handled.

## Research anchors

Declassified Official History of the 23rd Headquarters Special Troops; Ghost Army Legacy Project archive; U.S. Congressional Gold Medal findings; National WWII Museum.
