# EP01 — ศึกปราสาทอิตเทอร์: วันที่ศัตรูยืนอยู่ข้างเดียวกัน

**Event:** Battle of Castle Itter  
**Date:** 4–5 May 1945  
**Place:** Tyrol, Austria  
**Target:** 75 scenes x 8s ≈ 10 min, 9:16

## Hook

เปิดด้วยภาพทหารอเมริกันและทหารเยอรมันบางส่วนยืนอยู่บนกำแพงปราสาทเดียวกัน แล้วถามว่าเหตุใดอดีตศัตรูจึงต้องร่วมป้องกันสถานที่เดียวกันในช่วงวันสุดท้ายของสงครามยุโรป

## 5-act structure

- **Scenes 01–15 — Cold open + prison context:** Castle Itter, French VIP prisoners, Nazi system collapsing.
- **Scenes 16–30 — Guards disappear + calls for help:** castle becomes exposed; separate attempts to reach outside forces.
- **Scenes 31–45 — Gangl meets Lee:** local resistance context, Gangl’s choice, contact with U.S. armor, small relief force moves toward Itter.
- **Scenes 46–60 — Defense and siege:** defenders take positions, Sherman Besotten Jenny at the gate, Waffen-SS attack, tank disabled, ammunition pressure.
- **Scenes 61–75 — Gangl falls + Borotra + relief:** Gangl dies during the defense, Jean Borotra leaves to help connect with relief, American reinforcements arrive, aftermath.

## Principal roles

- Major Josef Gangl — German officer cooperating with Austrian resistance; defender at Itter.
- 1st Lt. John C. Lee Jr. — U.S. tank officer leading the small American force.
- Jean Borotra — French prisoner who later left the castle to help contact relief forces.
- Paul Reynaud — prominent French prisoner.
- Kurt-Siegfried Schrader — Waffen-SS officer at the castle who participated in the defense.
- Narrator — voice only; primary storyteller.

## Locations

Castle Itter, ramparts, main gate, Tyrol mountain roads, Wörgl, route toward Kufstein, surrounding woods.

## Accuracy notes

- Do not imply the Wehrmacht broadly “changed sides”; focus on the specific group and circumstances.
- Do not state an unverified intent that the attacking SS force came specifically to execute the prisoners.
- Avoid invented direct quotations.
- Keep final castle battle-worn but not depicted as total ruins.
