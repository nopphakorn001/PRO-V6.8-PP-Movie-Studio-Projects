# EP06 — เรือประมงปลอมกับระเบิดในท่าเรือสิงคโปร์

**Event:** Operation Jaywick  
**Period:** 2 September–19 October 1943; harbor raid 26 September 1943  
**Place:** Australia–Singapore route / Singapore Harbour  
**Target:** 75 scenes x 8s ≈ 10 min, 9:16

## Hook

เรือไม้ที่ดูเหมือนเรือประมงธรรมดาเดินทางหลายพันไมล์เข้าใกล้สิงคโปร์ที่ถูกญี่ปุ่นยึด ก่อนชายหกคนพายเรือพับเข้าไปในท่าเรือกลางคืนเพื่อติดทุ่นระเบิดกับเรือโดยไม่เปิดเผยตัว

## 5-act structure

- **Scenes 01–15 — Singapore under occupation + the idea:** why Allied planners wanted a covert strike, introduction to Z Special Unit and MV Krait.
- **Scenes 16–30 — Voyage in disguise:** departure from Exmouth, Krait disguised as a regional fishing vessel, engine trouble and long transit through waters controlled by Japan.
- **Scenes 31–45 — Forward base + folboats:** reach Subor/Panjang area, unload gear, six raiders prepare collapsible canoes and move toward Singapore.
- **Scenes 46–60 — Harbor raid:** night paddling, split into pairs, approach shipping, attach limpet mines, withdraw before explosions; avoid over-compressing multiple actions into one shot.
- **Scenes 61–75 — Escape and return:** ships are sunk/damaged, canoeists rendezvous with Krait, tense return including encounter with Japanese vessel, arrival back at Exmouth on 19 October, legacy and later consequences distinguished from Jaywick itself.

## Principal roles

- Major Ivan Lyon — commander.
- Hubert Carse — Krait commander / naval role.
- Five additional canoe raiders — cast from source-verified names.
- Krait crew — supporting ensemble.
- Narrator — primary storyteller.

## Locations

MV Krait deck/interior, open sea, island hide/base, mangrove/coastal approaches, Singapore Harbour at night, return route through Indonesian waters.

## Accuracy notes

- Australian War Memorial records six men paddling three two-man folboats into the harbor.
- AWM states seven ships were sunk or badly damaged; use “seven ships sunk or damaged” unless a specific source provides classification.
- Keep operation distinct from the later Operation Rimau and from reprisals/investigations that followed Jaywick.
- Avoid portraying disguises in a way that caricatures Malay people; focus on vessel appearance and operational cover.

## Research anchors

Australian War Memorial Operation Jaywick article, MV Krait history, photographs and digitised voyage log.
