# EP04 — คืนบุกโรงงานน้ำมวลหนัก

**Event:** Operation Gunnerside  
**Date:** Night of 27–28 February 1943  
**Place:** Vemork, Norway  
**Target:** 75 scenes x 8s ≈ 10 min, 9:16

## Hook

กลางฤดูหนาวของนอร์เวย์ หน่วยก่อวินาศกรรมต้องข้ามภูเขาและหุบเขาเพื่อเข้าโรงงานที่ฝ่ายสัมพันธมิตรกังวลว่ามีความสำคัญต่อโครงการนิวเคลียร์ของเยอรมนี — โดยเป้าหมายคือทำลายอุปกรณ์แล้วหายไปก่อนถูกจับได้

## 5-act structure

- **Scenes 01–15 — Why heavy water mattered:** Vemork plant, German occupation, Allied fears, earlier intelligence and the failed Freshman attempt as brief context.
- **Scenes 16–30 — Gunnerside arrives:** Norwegian team lands on Hardangervidda, blizzard and difficult movement, link-up with the earlier Grouse/Swallow group.
- **Scenes 31–45 — Approach to Vemork:** reconnaissance, choosing the gorge route instead of the guarded bridge, descent and movement toward the plant.
- **Scenes 46–60 — Inside the plant:** entry, explosives placed on production cells, evacuation from the target, explosion destroys cells and heavy-water stock; no conventional firefight.
- **Scenes 61–75 — Escape and limits of success:** members ski away toward Sweden or remain in Norway; production later resumes, placing Gunnerside within the broader heavy-water campaign rather than claiming it single-handedly ended a German atomic bomb program.

## Principal roles

- Joachim Rønneberg — Gunnerside leader.
- Norwegian sabotage team members — source-verified identities before final casting.
- Local resistance / Grouse-Swallow members.
- Narrator — primary storyteller.

## Locations

Hardangervidda plateau, snow shelters, mountain routes, Vemork gorge, railway line/gate, heavy-water plant exterior, production-cell basement/interior.

## Accuracy notes

- Official Norwegian sources describe the sabotage itself as completed without gunfire and without casualties among the raiders.
- Sources report destruction of the production cells and roughly 500 kg of heavy water; verify exact wording before narration.
- Avoid the simplistic claim “they stopped Hitler’s atomic bomb.” Explain the uncertainty and wider campaign.
- Keep winter equipment, skis, terrain and factory geometry historically plausible.

## Research anchors

Norwegian Industrial Workers Museum (NIA); Norwegian Armed Forces history; Norwegian Royal House commemorative material.
