# EP07 — คืนที่เดนมาร์กพาคนกว่าพันชีวิตข้ามทะเล

**Event:** Rescue of the Danish Jews  
**Period:** Late September–October 1943  
**Place:** Denmark–Sweden  
**Target:** 75 scenes x 8s ≈ 10 min, 9:16

## Hook

เมื่อข่าวแผนจับกุมชาวยิวในเดนมาร์กรั่วไหล ช่วงเวลาเพียงไม่กี่สัปดาห์เปลี่ยนบ้าน โบสถ์ โรงพยาบาล ถนน และเรือประมงให้กลายเป็นเครือข่ายช่วยคนหลบหนีข้ามทะเลไปยังสวีเดน

## 5-act structure

- **Scenes 01–15 — Occupied Denmark:** brief occupation context, Danish Jewish community, changing German policy and looming arrests.
- **Scenes 16–30 — Warning spreads:** information about the planned roundup reaches Danish contacts; families are warned, leave homes and hide with friends, institutions and strangers.
- **Scenes 31–45 — Moving to the coast:** resistance members and ordinary citizens organize routes, temporary hiding places, transport and contacts with fishermen.
- **Scenes 46–60 — Crossings to Sweden:** small boats depart at night across the Øresund; tension comes from patrols, darkness, weather and uncertainty rather than invented action scenes.
- **Scenes 61–75 — What was saved and what was not:** more than 7,000 Jews reach Sweden along with hundreds of non-Jewish relatives; hundreds are still arrested and deported to Theresienstadt; conclude with the importance and limits of the rescue.

## Principal roles

Use a small number of documented rescuers/families only after source verification. Most scenes should use historically plausible civilian ensembles rather than inventing a single fictional “hero.” Narrator is primary.

## Locations

Copenhagen streets/apartments, synagogue/community spaces, hospitals/churches or temporary hiding places where source-supported, coastal villages, fishing boats, Øresund at night, Swedish shore.

## Accuracy notes

- USHMM states more than 7,000 Jews and close to 700 non-Jewish relatives were ferried to Sweden in a little over three weeks.
- Also acknowledge roughly 500 Jews were arrested and deported to Theresienstadt; do not turn the event into an uncomplicated triumph.
- Avoid implying every Dane participated or that one centralized plan controlled all rescues; many efforts were spontaneous and networked.
- No invented Nazi dialogue or melodramatic executions.

## Research anchors

United States Holocaust Memorial Museum materials on the Danish rescue; Danish archival/museum sources for individual stories before final STEP6 casting.
